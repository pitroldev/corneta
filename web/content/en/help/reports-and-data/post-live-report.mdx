## Open the right stream

1. Go to **Reports**.
2. Pick the date and time.
3. Check the platforms, duration and quality mode.
4. Read the diagnosis notice before the charts.

<ContentImage baseName="corneta-post-live-report-en" />

Peak and average audience, messages and alerts only show up when that source delivered data. Missing data doesn't turn into a made-up zero.

## Read the diagnosis as a hypothesis

Corneta cross-checks CPU, GPU, OBS stats and the state of each destination. It can say “a quick stutter while preparing video” because the counters changed together; it can't see your router, your viewers' computers or every platform failure.

## Download the right format

- **HTML**: a readable report to open or attach.
- **CSV**: a table for comparing streams.
- **JSON**: structured data for technical analysis.
- **Recap**: an image ready to share, after you review it.

## If the numbers look wrong

| Symptom                             | Check                    | Likely explanation                          |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| missing audience                    | account connections      | the source didn't provide samples           |
| followers differ from the dashboard | period and attribution   | external events may lack a reliable channel |
| chart ends early                    | end time                 | the session or the app was interrupted      |
| cause doesn't match what you saw    | minute and other metrics | incomplete correlation                      |

Before sharing, check channel names, messages and any personal data. For technical support, use the [safe diagnostics file](/en/help/reports-and-data/export-safe-diagnostic) instead.
