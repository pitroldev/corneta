## Arm it before the stream

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Safety nets**.
3. Turn on **Drop protection (BE RIGHT BACK)**.
4. Confirm the **ARMED** badge.
5. Choose **Use Corneta's default** or **Pick a file of mine** (image or video).

<ContentImage baseName="corneta-live-safety-settings-en" />

With your own file, Corneta copies the chosen media into its local settings. Video plays in a loop and can have sound. Check the preview before GO LIVE.

## Test the return

Do an unlisted stream, wait for the signal to come in and close your streaming software. The destination should switch to **BE RIGHT BACK on air** without disconnecting. Open the software again and confirm your content comes back.

## Common symptoms

| Symptom                               | Likely cause                                                                              | Fix                                                   |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| destination on **Waiting for signal** | the software hasn't streamed yet; BE RIGHT BACK only kicks in after the first real signal | start streaming in the software                       |
| screen doesn't come up after a drop   | the protection wasn't armed                                                               | end the stream, arm it and run a new test             |
| your own file doesn't open            | the format, or the file can't be accessed                                                 | go back to the default screen and choose another file |
| content comes back without audio      | the audio hasn't arrived yet or a manual pause is on                                      | check the software and click **I'm back!**            |

BE RIGHT BACK doesn't cover your internet, your power or the whole PC. Don't share a file with personal data, and don't use a secret as part of the artwork.
