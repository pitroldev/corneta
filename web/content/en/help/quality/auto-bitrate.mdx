## Turn it on and check the scope

1. Open **Settings → Safety nets**.
2. Turn on **Hold the stream up when your internet chokes (auto-bitrate)**.
3. Confirm **ARMED**.
4. In **Quality**, see which platforms will be re-encoded.

<ContentImage baseName="corneta-live-safety-settings-en" />

When sending gets tight, Corneta lowers the bitrate of a re-encoded output and tries to raise it again once the connection steadies. It would rather degrade the picture for a while than let that destination drop.

## When it doesn't step in

The adjustment doesn't act on destinations in **Straight up** mode or on copies inside **Smart**. On those, the video arrives already encoded. To lower that output, lower the bitrate in your streaming software or switch the destination to re-encoding.

## What the adjustment doesn't fix on its own

The controller watches whether the output keeps up with the stream's pace; a slow output doesn't prove the cause is the internet. Lowering the bitrate doesn't fix an invalid key or URL, doesn't bring back a platform that's down, and doesn't restore a lost connection. It also isn't guaranteed to fix encoder overload or rendering problems in OBS: in those cases, check your machine's load and settings.

## How to confirm

If a destination reconnects even with auto-bitrate on, check whether it was being re-encoded and whether other outputs also lost their connection. Export the time and state through the diagnostic; never send a key or a URL with credentials.
