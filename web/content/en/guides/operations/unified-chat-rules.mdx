## The private panel is the safe default

Use the unified chat to read, reply and moderate without switching windows. Check the selector before you send: you can pick a channel or **All**, which sends to the channels available for sending. To reply on just one source, select that channel.

<ContentImage baseName="corneta-unified-chat-en" />

On Twitch, the simulcasting documentation allows tools that combine activity for personal use. That fits the panel and the floating window that stay only on your monitor.

## The overlay is a different decision

When the feed becomes a **Browser Source**, a message written on one platform shows up inside the video streamed to the others. Before you turn it on:

- keep the platform icon visible;
- hide commands that start with `!`;
- set a message limit and a fade-out time;
- test moderation and long names;
- let your community know when that context matters.

Don't use the overlay as a way to send the audience of one stream to another. On Twitch, actively promoting the simultaneous stream somewhere else falls under the simulcasting restrictions.

## Moderation doesn't carry over on its own

Deleting a message on Twitch doesn't guarantee it's removed from YouTube or Kick. The action applies on the channel and platform that accept that operation. The panel cuts down on alt-tabbing; it doesn't turn three moderation systems into one.

To put the feed in OBS with these options visible, follow [how to add the Corneta overlay](/en/help/chat-and-alerts/obs-browser-source-overlay).
