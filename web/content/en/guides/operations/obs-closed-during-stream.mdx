## With BE RIGHT BACK armed

Once the first real signal has arrived, Corneta can replace the missing video with the **BE RIGHT BACK** screen. The destinations stay connected and go back to your content when the signal returns.

<ContentImage baseName="corneta-live-safety-settings-en" />

This covers OBS closing, OBS restarting and an interruption of the local RTMP. There's also the **BE RIGHT BACK now** button, which puts the screen up manually and cuts the audio while you take a break.

## Without the protection

If the signal disappears in the middle of the stream and BE RIGHT BACK wasn't armed, Corneta warns you that it lost the input. The platforms may end the stream when they stop receiving media.

## What the protection doesn't cover

- a total internet outage;
- a PC that is shut down or frozen;
- Corneta being closed;
- a power outage;
- a block or shutdown by the platform.

It's a safety net inside the same PC, not a cloud relay. Covering a total failure of the machine would take another encoder somewhere else.

Test it first with an unlisted stream: go live, close OBS, confirm **BE RIGHT BACK on air** and open OBS again. The full procedure is in [how to use and customize the BE RIGHT BACK screen](/en/help/streaming-software/brb-screen).
