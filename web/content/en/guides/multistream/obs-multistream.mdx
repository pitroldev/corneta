## What is multistreaming in OBS Studio?

OBS builds scenes, combines audio and video and delivers one signal.
Multistreaming means sending that signal to two or more destinations, such as
Twitch, YouTube and Kick.

The difference is who does the distribution. In local mode, each destination
uses part of your upload. With a cloud relay, your PC sends one output and the
service replicates the signal out there.

## Three ways to distribute the stream

### Local app

OBS sends the signal to an app on the same computer. The app copies or adapts
the stream for each platform. That's how Corneta works: one destination can
drop, pause or reconnect without pulling the others down with it.

<ContentImage baseName="corneta-platform-destinations-en" />

### OBS plugin

A plugin adds new outputs inside OBS. It's the shortest path if you only want to
distribute the signal, but per-destination control and diagnostics depend on the
plugin you choose.

### Cloud relay

OBS sends a single output to an external service, which replicates the stream.
It makes sense when your upload can't handle the sum of the destinations or when
you'd rather take that load off your PC.

## How do you choose without guessing?

Run four checks:

1. add up the bitrate of every local output;
2. leave headroom for swings in your connection;
3. confirm whether the signal will be copied or re-encoded per destination;
4. decide whether you need to pause, reconnect and diagnose each platform
   independently.

<ContentImage baseName="corneta-quality-modes-en" />

If the math doesn't work out, don't fight the router. Lower the quality or use a
cloud relay. If your PC can't handle re-encoding, copy a compatible signal or
lower the resolution and FPS.

## Where Corneta fits in

Corneta sits between OBS and the platforms. OBS keeps handling production;
Corneta distributes, monitors and reconnects each destination.

## The ground rules before GO LIVE

- each local destination eats part of your upload;
- re-encoding uses CPU or GPU;
- each platform sets its own rules and limits;
- Corneta runs on Windows first, and OBS keeps producing the stream;
- with little upload, a cloud relay may be the right choice.
