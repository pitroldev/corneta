## Before setup: check your accounts

Twitch allows simulcasting unless you have an individual exclusivity agreement.
The experience on Twitch can't be worse, and you can't use your Twitch stream to
push the audience to another, competing stream.

YouTube needs to be verified and have live streaming enabled. The first
activation can take at least 24 hours.

If you're in the **KICK Partner Program**, Kick tells you to turn on the
**Multistreaming** toggle after starting your stream or to use `/multi on`.
During that time, the program's payout is reduced by 50%. This rule is specific
to the program and may change; check your dashboard before going live.

## Add the three destinations

1. Open **Platforms** in Corneta.
2. Add Twitch, paste the stream key and click **Test the server**.
3. Do the same with YouTube.
4. Add Kick and confirm the URL shown in its dashboard.
5. Get all three cards to **Ready**.

<ContentImage baseName="corneta-platform-destinations-en" />

Don't reuse a key across platforms. Each key identifies an account and can
expire or be revoked without affecting the others.

## Do the upload math

Add up the video and audio bitrate of the three outputs. If each one uses
6 Mbps, the floor is already close to 18 Mbps before headroom. YouTube suggests
aiming for 1.5 to 2 times the combined bitrate for stable simulstreaming.

If the math doesn't work out, lower the bitrates or use a relay. Cutting your
headroom to make it "fit" usually shows up as dropped frames when the scene
moves.

## See how many re-encodes will open

**Straight up** copies the same signal to all three and is the lightest on your
machine, as long as all three accept the output from OBS. **Smart** adapts only
the incompatible destinations. **All out** re-encodes each one and can open
three encoder sessions.

<ContentImage baseName="corneta-quality-modes-en" />

Run a test with the game, camera and alerts. If the GPU, CPU or encoder hits
its limit, reduce the load before blindly cutting quality.

## When it's time to hit GO LIVE

1. Confirm the Multistreaming toggle on Kick if your account is in the Partner
   Program.
2. Hit **GO LIVE** and wait for all three statuses to move past **Connecting**.
3. Open each channel on another device and check picture and audio.
4. If one destination reconnects, keep the others on air and deal only with the
   output that has the problem.

## A checklist that prevents the most common scare

- no key shows up in a screenshot or screen share;
- Twitch gets equivalent quality;
- there's no CTA on Twitch sending people to another, competing stream;
- YouTube was already enabled before showtime;
- you've checked the current rule for your Kick account;
- upload and encoder sessions have headroom.
