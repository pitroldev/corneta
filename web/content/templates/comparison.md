# Template: comparison

Copy to `{locale}/guides/comparisons/{english-slug}.mdx` and use
`kind: comparison`.

Besides the full frontmatter from `guide.md`, the body must record:

1. the question and the audience of the comparison;
2. date, versions, plans and environment;
3. a hands-on methodology with the same task for every alternative;
4. a factual matrix in HTML/Markdown, not as an image;
5. the scenario where each alternative is better;
6. the limitations of Corneta, before the call to action;
7. primary sources for pricing, rules and volatile specifications;
8. a history of the article's important corrections.

Do not publish a conclusion based only on landing pages or documentation. Do not
use arbitrary scores, invented testimonials or undated claims.
