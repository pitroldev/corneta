# Third-party notices

Corneta's code is distributed under the MIT license. The installer includes components with
their own licenses; the application's MIT license does not replace those obligations.

## FFmpeg

The `scripts/fetch-binaries.ps1` script pins a GPL build of FFmpeg provided by
BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds. When distributing the installer that contains this binary, publish with it the
GPL notices, the corresponding source code and the configuration/build information required
by the license. Do not publish the installer before this compliance package is complete.

The pipeline preserves licenses and `-buildconf` in the installer. `pnpm compliance:prepare` generates
`corneta-third-party.zip` with sources verified by SHA-256 and Rust/JS inventories. Before that,
fill in and review `compliance/ffmpeg-sources.json` for the exact build; the manifest deliberately
starts as not approved. The automation does not replace reviewing that the sources correspond.

- Project: https://ffmpeg.org/
- Build used and hash: documented in `scripts/fetch-binaries.ps1`
- License: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

## MediaMTX

MediaMTX is downloaded from a pinned release and verified by SHA-256 by the same script. Preserve
its license/notice file in the distribution.

- Project: https://github.com/bluenviron/mediamtx

## Rust and JavaScript dependencies

The exact dependencies are in `src-tauri/Cargo.lock` and `pnpm-lock.yaml`. CI runs
`cargo audit` and `cargo deny check`; a release must attach the license inventory generated
for the published version.

## Fonts and icons

The Inter and Baloo 2 families come from the `@fontsource-variable/inter`
and `@fontsource/baloo-2` packages, under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**, with their
authors' notices in the `LICENSE` files of the installed packages. Preserve those
files and notices when distributing the assets; do not associate the fonts with the code's MIT license.

Lucide declares **ISC** and includes **MIT** notices for the icons derived from Feather.
Simple Icons declares **CC0-1.0**, which does not remove trademark and other rights
in the symbols depicted. The release inventory must preserve the full notices
of these packages, not just the license name.

## Original art and screenshots

The Corneta icons and the installer art are generated by
`scripts/make-icons.ps1`, `scripts/make-tray-icons.ps1` and
`scripts/make-installer-art.ps1`; the interface geometry is also in
`src/components/decor.tsx`. The editorial manifest in `web/content/assets/manifest.json`
records the interface screenshots and their demo data. See the
[public assets review](docs/PUBLIC-ASSETS.md).

Names/logos of Twitch, YouTube, Kick, OBS and other services identify integrations;
the project does not claim ownership of them or official affiliation. Emotes and other
platform elements seen in the chat do not become Corneta's work by
appearing in a screenshot. The code license does not grant third-party trademark rights.

## Distribution compliance evidence

The status of the verified FFmpeg sources and of what is still missing is in
[FFMPEG-COMPLIANCE.md](docs/FFMPEG-COMPLIANCE.md). Partial sources and correct hashes
are not enough to approve the set. The manifest stays not approved until
the full correspondence and the distribution notices have been checked.
